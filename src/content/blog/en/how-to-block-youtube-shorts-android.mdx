**YouTube has no setting that actually turns Shorts off.** The toggle that hides the Shorts shelf
from your homepage is cosmetic — the feed is still one search, notification, or shared link away,
and Google has moved or removed that toggle more than once between releases.

What works instead is a **feed-level limit**: cap the Shorts feed itself with a daily or
per-session limit, and leave the rest of YouTube alone. Tutorials, subscriptions, playlists, and
long-form video keep working exactly as before. This guide sets that up on Android in about two
minutes, with no root and no VPN.

## Why "just block YouTube" is bad advice

A whole-app blocker treats YouTube as a single on/off switch. Block it during focus hours and you
also lose:

- The tutorial you needed for work
- Your subscriptions and watch-later queue
- Podcasts and long-form video you chose to watch

That is why whole-app blocks on YouTube rarely survive more than a few days — the cost is too high,
so people disable the blocker the first time they need a real video. The fix is not giving up
YouTube. It is targeting the one feed inside it that is built to be infinite.

## Why the Shorts shelf toggle isn't enough

YouTube's own setting hides the Shorts row from your homepage. It does not stop the feed. Shorts
still reach you through:

- **Search results**, where Shorts appear alongside long-form video
- **Notifications** from channels that post Shorts
- **Shared links** from friends, which open the Shorts player directly
- **The subscriptions tab**, which mixes Shorts into the feed

Hiding a shelf changes what you see on one screen. It does not change what happens when you tap a
Short from anywhere else — which is where most Shorts sessions actually start.

## Daily limit vs. per-session limit

Two settings do the actual work, and you can use either or both:

- **Daily limit** — a total Shorts budget per day (e.g. 10 minutes). Once it is spent, Shorts is
  blocked until the next day, and the rest of YouTube stays open.
- **Per-session limit** — a cap on any single sitting (e.g. 3 minutes). This is what stops the
  scroll before it becomes a session, since the damage is rarely opening Shorts once — it is the
  half hour you did not plan to lose.

Set a hard limit and there is nothing to negotiate with. No "add 5 more minutes" button to tap.
