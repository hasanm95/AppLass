**Arisely is a free Android alarm clock built for heavy sleepers — the people who tap snooze in their sleep and wake up late.** Unlike a normal alarm, it keeps ringing at full volume until you complete a wake-up mission you choose, from solving math to doing camera-verified push-ups. It is published on Google Play by Applass Android Apps under the name *Arisely: Alarm + AI Push-Up*.

If you have ever turned off an alarm without remembering it, the problem is not your willpower. It is the off button. A single sleepy tap is all it takes, and your half-asleep brain is very good at finding it. Arisely's core idea is simple: **remove the easy off switch.**

## What makes Arisely different from a normal alarm app

Most alarm apps let you dismiss the sound with one tap. Arisely doesn't. When the alarm fires, you pick a mission, and the alarm **keeps ringing at full volume until you actually finish it.** There is no "slide to stop," so there is nothing to tap by reflex.

Three engineering choices make that work in practice:

- **Exact-time alarms.** Arisely uses exact alarms that fire even when the phone is locked, idle, or the app has been closed for hours — not batched, delayed background tasks. The alarm goes off when you set it, every time.
- **The alarm audio channel.** The sound plays on Android's dedicated alarm channel, so it rings at full volume even when your media is muted or Do Not Disturb is on. There is an extra-loud boost for the heaviest sleepers.
- **A mission gate.** The sound stops only when the task is complete, and the tasks require real attention — which is exactly what a half-asleep brain lacks.

## The 10 wake-up missions

You match the mission to how deeply you sleep. Light sleeper? A quick math problem is enough. Sleep through a fire drill? Put a barcode across the room, or make yourself do push-ups.

| Mission | What you do to turn the alarm off |
| :------ | :-------------------------------- |
| **Math** | Solve a quick problem |
| **Memory** | Watch a pattern, then repeat it back |
| **Code** | Retype a random code exactly |
| **Sequence** | Find the next number in the pattern |
| **Typing** | Retype a short phrase exactly |
| **Trivia** | Answer a multiple-choice question correctly |
| **Shake** | Shake your phone a set number of times |
| **Step** | Take a set number of steps |
| **QR / Barcode** | Scan a code you placed near your bed |
| **AI Push-Up** | Do a set number of push-ups in front of the camera |

## The AI Push-Up Mission

This is the feature most alarm apps don't have. You set a push-up count, and **Arisely's camera checks your form in real time.** You can't fake the reps and you can't skip them. It is the strongest option in the app because it gets your body moving and your heart rate up before you are fully awake — and once you are physically up and moving, falling back into bed is a conscious decision, not a reflex.

## Is Arisely private?

Yes. Arisely is designed to stay out of your business:

- **No account needed** — install it and set an alarm. There is no sign-up and no email.
- **No data collected or shared.** Its Google Play *Data safety* listing declares no data collected and no data shared with third parties. Everything you create — alarms, settings, sleep history — stays on your phone.

That matters for an app you grant alarm, camera, and motion access to. The camera used by the push-up mission checks your form on-device; there is no cloud account recording your mornings.
