## Why blocking Reels and Shorts is harder than blocking an app

Blocking a whole app is easy. Every basic blocker can do it. Blocking *only the short-form feed
inside* an app is the hard part, and it's the part that matters — because the feed is deliberately
woven into apps you have real reasons to keep.

- **Instagram** = messaging + Stories + posts + **Reels**
- **YouTube** = tutorials + music + subscriptions + **Shorts**
- **TikTok** = creator tools + DMs + **the For You feed**

Delete the app and you lose the useful 80% to kill the harmful 20%. That's why most people
uninstall, last four days, and reinstall. The trap isn't the app — it's the feed. So the fix has
to target the feed.

## The three ways to block short-form video on Android

**1. Feed-level limits (Reel Guard) — the surgical option.** A tool that detects the short-form
feed itself and blocks or limits it while the rest of the app keeps working. This is what actually
survives contact with real life, because you're not asked to give up messaging to escape the
scroll. Mindful Guard's **Reel Guard** works this way across Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts,
TikTok, Facebook Reels, and Snapchat Spotlight.

**2. Whole-app blocking — the blunt option.** Block Instagram, YouTube, or TikTok entirely during
focus hours. Effective for deep-work windows, overkill for all-day use. Best combined with option 1:
hard-block during work, feed-limit the rest of the day.

**3. Built-in tools (Digital Wellbeing, app timers) — the weak option.** Android's Digital
Wellbeing and Instagram's own "Take a break" reminders show a nudge you can dismiss in one tap.
They raise *awareness*, but they don't enforce anything. If a three-second override is available,
your reflex will use it every time.

## How daily and per-session limits actually work

The reason a hard limit beats a reminder is that it removes the negotiation. Two levers do the
work:

- **Daily limit** — a total budget per platform per day (e.g. 15 minutes of Reels). Spend it
  however you like; when it's gone, the feed is blocked until tomorrow.
- **Per-session limit** — a cap on any single sitting (e.g. 5 minutes at a time). This is the one
  that breaks the *doomscroll* specifically, because the damage isn't opening Reels — it's the
  40-minute session you didn't decide to have.

Set one, or set both, **separately for each platform.** When the limit is up, it's up — no "just
one more," no "add 5 minutes." That's the entire point.
