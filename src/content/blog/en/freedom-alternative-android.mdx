## Does Freedom work on Android?

Yes — Freedom publishes an Android app on Google Play (`to.freedom.android2`), and it works. Some
comparison sites still claim Freedom is desktop-only; that is out of date.

The detail that matters is *how* it works. On Android, Freedom enforces blocks through what it
describes as a limited VPN profile that filters traffic on the device. That is a reasonable
engineering choice for a cross-platform product — a VPN layer behaves roughly the same everywhere —
but it is the root of the Android-specific complaints below.

## Where Freedom's Android app costs you something

**It runs on a VPN.** Blocking works by routing traffic through a local VPN tunnel and filtering it
there. Effective, but holding that tunnel open has a real cost: users report the phone warming up
and the battery draining noticeably faster during sessions, and connections becoming unstable the
longer a session runs — especially on older devices and phones with aggressive battery management.
On Samsung, Xiaomi and OnePlus devices, the OS killing a VPN tunnel to save power means the block
silently stops.

**It requires an account.** You sign up with an email and password, and sessions sync through
Freedom's servers so devices stay in step. That is genuinely useful if you are blocking a laptop and
a phone at once. It also means usage data passes through a server you do not control, in exchange
for a feature you may not need.

**The free plan is capped hard.** The free tier covers unlimited devices, custom blocklists and
cross-device sync, but sessions are "Start Now" only and capped at 2 hours, with no recurring or
advance scheduling. Locked Mode — the feature that stops you quitting mid-session, which is arguably
the entire point of a blocker — is Premium-only.

## On price: the comparison people get wrong

Mindful Guard Premium is $3.99 per month. Over a year that is more than Freedom's $39.96 annual
plan, and any page telling you otherwise is doing arithmetic it hopes you will not check.

The real difference is *where the paywall sits*. The two features most people upgrade Freedom for —
scheduled blocks and a locked mode you cannot quit — are free in Mindful Guard, within a limit of
three focus sessions per day.

So the practical comparison depends on your routine. If three blocks a day covers it — a morning
block, an afternoon block, an evening block — you are comparing "free" against "$39.96/year." If you
need more than three, you are comparing "$47.88" against "$39.96," and Freedom is cheaper. Either
way, Mindful Guard is month-to-month rather than an annual commitment.
