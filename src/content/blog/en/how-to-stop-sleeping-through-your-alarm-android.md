**To stop sleeping through your alarm on Android, you have to fix three separate causes at once: sleep inertia, the one-tap snooze reflex, and Android delaying the alarm.** A louder ringtone only touches one of them, which is why buying a new tone rarely works. Here's each cause and its fix.

## Why you sleep through it

| Cause | What's happening | The fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sleep inertia** | In deep sleep your brain is groggy enough to dismiss an alarm with no memory of it | Require a task that needs real attention |
| **The off button** | One sleepy tap silences it before you're conscious | Remove the one-tap escape |
| **Android delay** | Battery optimization / Doze delays or mutes background-scheduled alarms | Use exact-time alarms |

## Fix 1: Make the alarm actually fire — exact-time alarms

If your alarm sometimes goes off late or not at all, Android's battery optimizer is likely batching it. Alarms scheduled as ordinary background work can be delayed. Apps that use **exact alarms** fire at the precise time you set, even when the phone is locked, idle, or the app has been closed for hours. This is the foundation — nothing else matters if the alarm never rings.

## Fix 2: Make it loud enough to break through

Set an alarm that plays on Android's **dedicated alarm audio channel**, not the media channel. That way it stays at full volume even when your media is muted or Do Not Disturb is on. Turn on any "extra loud" boost if you're a heavy sleeper.

## Fix 3: Remove the one-tap off button

This is the one most people miss. If silencing the alarm takes one tap, your half-asleep hand will find it. The fix is to put a **task between you and the off switch** — something that forces you awake:

- Solve a math problem
- Retype a code or short phrase
- Take a set number of steps
- Scan a QR/barcode you placed across the room
- Do camera-verified push-ups

Once you're doing a task, you're awake. That's the whole mechanism.

## Fix 4: Kill the snooze loop

Snoozing fragments your sleep and trains the dismiss-without-waking reflex. Set a **snooze limit**, or turn snooze off entirely so the only way out is the wake-up task.
