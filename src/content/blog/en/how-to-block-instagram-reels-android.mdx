## Why there's no real "off" switch for Reels

Instagram's Reels feed isn't a bolt-on you can disable, because it's core to how the app keeps you
scrolling:

- It appears as its own tab, but also surfaces inside your **main feed** between posts
- It's pushed through **Explore** and notification prompts
- **Shared Reels** from DMs and group chats bypass any tab-avoidance strategy entirely

That's why "just don't tap the Reels tab" fails as advice — the feed finds you. A real block has to
detect and stop the feed itself, not rely on you avoiding a button.

## Whole-app block vs. Reels-only block

Most people's first move is to block Instagram entirely during focus hours. It works, but it's
expensive: you lose DMs, Stories, and your feed along with Reels — which is why whole-app blocks on

## Daily limit vs. per-session limit for Reels

Two settings, use either or both:

- **Daily limit** — a total Reels budget per day (e.g. 15 minutes). Spend it however you like;
  once it's gone, Reels is blocked until tomorrow — the rest of Instagram stays open.
- **Per-session limit** — a cap on any single sitting (e.g. 5 minutes). This is the one that
  actually breaks the doomscroll, since the damage isn't opening Reels once — it's the 30-minute
  session that started as "just checking a DM."

A hard limit removes the negotiation entirely. There's no "5 more minutes" button to tap.
