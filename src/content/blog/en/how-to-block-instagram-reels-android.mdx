**Instagram has no "turn off Reels" switch.** Open the settings and look — you will not find one,
because Reels is not a feature you opt into. It is the feed Instagram wants you inside as often as
possible.

Avoiding the Reels tab does not work either, because the algorithm surfaces Reels inside your main
feed, in Explore, and through DMs. The fix is a **feed-level limit** that blocks the Reels feed
itself on your terms, while DMs, Stories, and posting — the parts you actually opened the app for —
keep working untouched.

## Why there's no real "off" switch for Reels

Instagram's Reels feed is not a bolt-on you can disable, because it is core to how the app keeps you
scrolling:

- It appears as its own tab, but also surfaces inside your **main feed** between posts
- It is pushed through **Explore** and notification prompts
- **Shared Reels** from DMs and group chats bypass any tab-avoidance strategy entirely

That is why "just don't tap the Reels tab" fails as advice — the feed finds you. A real block has to
detect and stop the feed itself, not rely on you avoiding a button.

Most people's first move is to block Instagram entirely during focus hours. It works, but it is

## Daily limit vs. per-session limit for Reels

Two settings, use either or both:

- **Daily limit** — a total Reels budget per day (e.g. 15 minutes). Spend it however you like;
  once it is gone, Reels is blocked until tomorrow, and the rest of Instagram stays open.
- **Per-session limit** — a cap on any single sitting (e.g. 5 minutes). This is the one that
  actually breaks the doomscroll, since the damage is not opening Reels once — it is the 30-minute
  session that started as "just checking a DM."

A hard limit removes the negotiation entirely. There is no "5 more minutes" button to tap.
