Freedom and Opal are the names most people have heard, and neither is in this list, for one reason:

## One thing to check before you install any of these

Every app here needs **accessibility service permission**, and that permission is genuinely powerful:
it lets an app read what is on your screen. That is exactly what feed detection requires, and it is
also why the permission is worth a moment's thought.

The meaningful safeguard is not a privacy policy — it is whether the app can transmit anything at
all. An app with **no internet permission** cannot send screen data anywhere, no matter what its
policy says or who later acquires it. Check the permissions list on the Play Store listing before
granting access. We wrote about why this distinction matters in
[the case for a zero-telemetry blocker](/blog/zero-telemetry-blocker).
