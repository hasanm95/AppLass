A few years ago, "block Reels and Shorts" meant either blocking the whole app or nothing at all.
That's no longer true. A real category of dedicated feed-level blockers has grown up on the Play
Store — apps that use Android's Accessibility Service to detect a short-form feed specifically and
block or limit it, while leaving the rest of the host app alone.

We looked at the most-reviewed apps in this category — ratings, download counts, what's actually
free versus paywalled, and which platforms each one covers — instead of assuming MindfulGuard has
no real competition. Here's the honest comparison, MindfulGuard included.

---

**Quick answer:** For a free daily-and-per-session limit system across five platforms with no
account and no paywall on that core mechanic, **MindfulGuard** is our pick. If you want the single
most-reviewed dedicated feed blocker, **NoScroll** (4.63★, 27K ratings, 1.2M downloads) and
**StopScroll** (4.74★, 7.7K ratings) are both excellent, genuinely competitive alternatives.

## MindfulGuard — best free daily + per-session limit system

MindfulGuard's Reel Guard is built around **two independent limits** — a daily budget and a
per-session cap — available on the free tier for all five platforms it covers, with no VPN, no
account, and no paywall on that core daily/session mechanic. It's also bundled with full app
blocking (Strict Mode), so you're not choosing between a feed blocker and an app blocker — it's one
free app doing both.

- **Best for:** People who specifically want the daily-limit-plus-session-limit combination (see our
  [session-limit method guide](/blog/limit-reels-per-day-android)) without hitting a paywall, and who
  also want whole-app blocking in the same tool.
- **Trade-off:** Newer to the category than some competitors here, so it has a smaller review base
  to date — the comparison above is about what the free tier includes, not how long each app has
  been around.

## NoScroll — most-reviewed, one-time unlock price

NoScroll is the most established app in this list by the numbers: 4.63 stars across roughly 27,000
ratings and 1.2 million downloads, live on the Play Store since late 2023. Its free tier covers
everyday Reels/Shorts blocking, and its **OneReel Mode** — letting you watch one short video at a
time instead of an endless feed — is a distinctive middle-ground feature between "blocked" and
"unlimited." Premium is a one-time payment (roughly €10) rather than a subscription.

- **Best for:** Anyone who wants the most battle-tested option and prefers paying once instead of a
  recurring subscription.
- **Trade-off:** Covers three platforms (Instagram, YouTube, TikTok) rather than five — no listed
  Facebook Reels or Snapchat Spotlight support.

## StopScroll — highest rating, built-in daily limit tab

StopScroll rates 4.74 stars across about 7,700 ratings, with a dedicated **Day Limit** tab alongside
its Block All and Break features. It covers five platforms, matching MindfulGuard's range.

- **Best for:** People who want a simple, high-rated app with a straightforward daily-limit view.
- **Trade-off:** The Break feature and deeper customization/analytics sit behind a subscription
  (roughly ₹100/month or ₹400/year), and some users report the block occasionally stopping and
  needing a restart.

## Shortstop — closest architecture to MindfulGuard

Shortstop is nearly a direct technical match for MindfulGuard's approach: Accessibility Service
only (no VPN), explicitly no account required, covering the same five platforms, plus full app
blocking as a bonus. Its signature features are a 10-minute "Mindful Pause" and a Strict Mode that
adds a cooldown before you can turn blocking off.

- **Best for:** Anyone who wants a no-account, no-VPN feed blocker with a cooldown-based strict mode
  rather than a hard daily/session cap.
- **Trade-off:** Pricing and premium details weren't publicly listed at the time of writing — check
  the Play Store listing for current terms.

## ScrollGuard — broadest platform coverage

ScrollGuard covers more ground than anything else here on Android: Instagram, YouTube, TikTok,
Facebook, Reddit, LinkedIn, and Snapchat, with iOS support (via web-app workarounds) in progress for
Snapchat. If your doomscroll problem extends to Reddit or LinkedIn feeds specifically, this is the
only app on this list that reaches those.

- **Best for:** People whose short-form-feed problem isn't limited to Instagram/YouTube/TikTok.
- **Trade-off:** Broader platform coverage on Android comes with a less mature iOS implementation
  (web-app based rather than native).

## How to choose

- **Want daily + per-session limits with nothing paywalled, plus whole-app blocking bundled in?**
  MindfulGuard.
- **Want the most-reviewed, one-time-payment option?** NoScroll.
- **Want the highest star rating and a simple daily-limit view?** StopScroll.
- **Want the closest match to MindfulGuard's no-account, no-VPN architecture?** Shortstop.
- **Need Reddit or LinkedIn feeds blocked too, not just Instagram/YouTube/TikTok?** ScrollGuard.

## Bottom line

None of these apps need a VPN or your data to work — they all lean on the same Accessibility
Service approach, which is a genuinely good sign for the category. The real differences are in the
details: what's actually free, how many platforms are covered, and whether the limit mechanic is a
hard daily-and-session cap or a softer pause/cooldown. Pick based on which of those matters most to
your actual habit, not just the name you've heard of first.
